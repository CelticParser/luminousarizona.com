## Where I Go To Scream
> _I chase the sun, a gilded, burning guide,
<br>Across the land to find the sapphire hue.
<br>To leave the clamor of the world outside,
<br>And walk until the sky is sharp and new.
<br><br>
I seek the silence of a lonely space,
<br>A canvas painted in cerulean light,
<br>Where I can find a solitary place,
<br>To hide from judgment in the day or night.
<br><br>
For in this vastness, I can finally speak,
<br>And let the fury that I hold erupt.
<br>
The winds will carry what I cannot keep,
<br>And all the pain the world has built will corrupt.
<br><br>
In beautiful blue, where endless oceans cry,
<br>I find a space to scream beneath the sky._
