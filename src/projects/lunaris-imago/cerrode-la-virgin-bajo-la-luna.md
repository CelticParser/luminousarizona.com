> _The silver moonlight bathes the desert crest, 
<br>Where Cerro de la Virgen greets the night, 
<br>A silent watcher waking from her rest, 
<br>To paint the rocky landscape in her light.
<br><br>
She lifts her right hand in the glowing air, 
<br>To point toward a Celtic cross of wood, 
<br>A monument of faith left standing there, 
<br>To symbolize a timeless brotherhood.
<br><br>
Her other hand extends across the sand, 
<br>To Arizona’s Mexican frontier, 
<br>Uniting two sides of a fractured land, 
<br>And making every distant shadow clear.
<br><br>
A mother's grace beneath the moon above, 
<br>Embracing both sides of the line with love._
